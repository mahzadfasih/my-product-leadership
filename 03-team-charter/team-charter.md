# Team Charter: Meridian Foundations

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The Foundations team owns the outcomes and prioritization of the shared Foundations roadmap, including daily log simplification, field-office continuity, and compliance automation. We are accountable for protecting the field adoption bet and ensuring platform and AI investments directly support measurable field and office workflow outcomes.
>
> **Out of scope:** PM 1 owns the mobile experience and field adoption roadmap; PM 2 owns enterprise-specific capabilities and renewal commitments. Engineering capacity should be allocated first to the shared Foundations bets that drive field adoption and workflow continuity; enterprise-specific requests receive incremental capacity only when they demonstrate strategic value without displacing committed field outcomes, or when the Foundations owner explicitly approves the trade-off.
>
> **Joint calls:** Any decision that changes shared architecture, platform dependencies, data access, compliance requirements, or workflows spanning field and enterprise requires agreement between the affected PMs and the Foundations owner before commitment. Any enterprise request that consumes capacity allocated to field adoption requires an explicit assessment of its impact on field success metrics and an agreed trade-off.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** The Foundations owner has final decision authority over shared initiative scope, engineering capacity allocation, and roadmap trade-offs; PM 1 decides field experience priorities within the agreed allocation, and PM 2 decides enterprise experience priorities within theirs. Enterprise Sales can surface renewal risks and business urgency but cannot commit engineering capacity or unilaterally reprioritize the shared roadmap.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** The PM raising the conflict must document the customer or business impact, requested scope change, engineering capacity required, and consequences for existing roadmap commitments, then bring it to the Foundations owner and affected PM within one business day. The Foundations owner facilitates a decision within two business days, making the final call based on initiative-level outcomes and explicit trade-offs; unresolved disagreements are escalated to the relevant product leadership for review, but the existing roadmap allocation remains in effect until a decision is made.
